Meditation

Luke 17:26–29, Noah and Lot: the Catechism on the great flood and the Red Sea as figures of baptism.

The waters of the great flood you made a sign of the waters of Baptism, that make an end of sin and a new beginning of goodness. . . . But above all, the crossing of the Red Sea, literally the liberation of Israel from the slavery of Egypt, announces the liberation wrought by Baptism: You freed the children of Abraham from the slavery of Pharaoh, bringing them dry-shod through the waters of the Red Sea, to be an image of the people set free in Baptism.

— Catechism of the Catholic Church, §§1219, 1221 (baptism prefigured)
